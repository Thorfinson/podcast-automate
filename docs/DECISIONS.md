# Decisions

All decisions and open questions of podcast-automate. IDs never change; a replaced decision keeps its row with
`superseded by D-nn`. The plans in [specs/](specs/) hold the context of their dates.

## Decisions

| D-168 | 2026-10-07 | A podcast can be made again as a new version: a new project beside the old one (`project_versions.py`, `pla new-version`, „Neue Version anlegen“ in an overview card's menu), named `<topic>-v<n>-<id>` and numbered per podcast in its `version.json`. An approval belongs to the material it was given for, so none travels. | accepted |
