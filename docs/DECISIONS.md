# Decisions

All decisions and open questions of podcast-automate. IDs never change; a replaced decision keeps its row with
`superseded by D-nn`. The plans in [specs/](specs/) hold the context of their dates.

## Decisions

### Product and scope

Seven Ontologies episodes of 33 to 37 minutes had come in two parts. | accepted |
| D-153 | 2026-10-07 | Everything a podcast's listeners and readers get follows the project's content language (`TopicBrief.language`), whatever the Studio's interface language: download and ZIP names, the export's README, transcript notice, show notes, listening sheet, companion kit, series outline, teaching plan, `research_needed.md` and the readable research files (`content_text.py`: `de-DE` and `en-US`, any other language German). | D-156 | 2026-10-07 | The command line is English: help texts, metavars (such as `--dispute OBJECTION_ID SIDE`), prints, the CLI's own messages and the doctor's detail texts; text output reads `OK` or `MISSING`.
