# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
with the adaptations below. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
advisories, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, also to people who are new to Python, podcasts or AI models.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and no publishing of
  someone else's private information, such as an address, an e-mail address or a key, without their permission.

The Covenant's full text defines the expected and unacceptable behaviour and is part of this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer privately through the repository's **Security** tab: choose **Report a
vulnerability** and start the title with `Conduct:`. This is the only private channel the repository offers; it is
seen only by you and the maintainer. Content that breaks GitHub's own rules can also be reported to GitHub with
**Report content** on the comment or issue.

Every report is reviewed and answered as soon as possible; the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer enforces this Code of Conduct along the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact. Comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break it may be edited, hidden or removed.
