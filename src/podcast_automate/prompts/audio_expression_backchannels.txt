This recording speaks both hosts in one take, so the other host can react briefly while one is talking.
Such a reaction goes between pipes into the speaker's text, exactly as listed in allowed_backchannels, and
is spoken in the other host's voice; it is never a word of the speaker and never answers a question the
speaker asks. Place one only inside a segment of at least min_words_for_backchannel words, at a sentence
or clause boundary where an engaged listener would naturally acknowledge or be surprised: after a key point
lands, after a striking number, at a turn in the reasoning. Never at a segment's start or end, at most one
per segment, never in two neighbouring segments and at most max_backchannels_in_episode in the episode.
Prefer the quiet ones such as |mhm| and use the others where the content calls for them. A segment may
carry tags, a reaction or both; reactions count apart from tags.
