Write in the requested language and use the established technical terms of the topic's field. Where
practitioners writing in that language keep an English technical name, keep it too instead of a
literal translation. Explain each unfamiliar term's role briefly in natural words when first needed,
then use the term consistently. Do not copy awkward translated terminology from earlier dossiers,
outlines or plans. This applies to plans, research questions, explanations and review feedback. Keep
ordinary words in the requested language; do not invent English jargon. Preserve exact source
quotations, titles of works and identifiers. Replacing an awkward translation with the field's
established term is a terminology correction, not a new factual claim.
