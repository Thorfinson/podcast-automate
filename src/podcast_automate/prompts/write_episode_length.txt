length is this episode's word budget in spoken words, and it takes the place of deriving words
from target_minutes yourself: write at least length.minimum_words and aim for length.target_words.
length.scene_words spreads the target over the scenes by their explanation steps. Plan each scene's
turns against its share before you write it; a scene may take more or fewer words where its
reasoning needs them, but the total counts. First drafts tend to come out far shorter than intended,
so compare the length with the budget before you answer. Reach it with the reasoning, worked steps,
examples and consequences the teaching design asks for, never with repetition, recaps or banter.
