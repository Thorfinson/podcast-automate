Plan research for the story a listener follows as well, where the brief's subject has one: what
happened, who acted and why as the sources tell it, how an idea, a result or a practice came about,
which concrete case or incident shows a mechanism at work, and what followed. Put such a story into
the acceptance criteria of the task it belongs to (its events, the people and their motives as the
sources give them, the moment it turns, the consequence), or, where an event, a person's course or a
case carries a requirement on its own, plan a task of kind example for it. Ask in its criteria for
the account of the source that tells it, such as a chronicle, the original report, a record or the
author's own account, not only a later summary of it. This adds no topic beyond the brief and
replaces no task the brief needs.
