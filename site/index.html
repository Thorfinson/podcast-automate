From a topic to a researched dialogue podcast

Podcast Automate reads sources, builds an evidence-backed dossier, has a Claude Code agent write each episode as a dialogue of two or three hosts, reviews it against the sources and, once you have read and approved it, records it with synthetic voices. Open source, on your own computer.
